      <h2>How your own conditions get settled</h2>
      <p>Entry permission depends on the passport you hold and the dates you travel, so it is answered per case rather than as one blanket rule for all visitors.</p>
    </div>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li>
        <h3>1. You tell us the passport and the window</h3>
        <p>Send the nationality on your passport and the dates you expect to be on island, in the inquiry form or the channel you chose.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>2. We answer from the government page that governs it</h3>
        <p>Each condition is read from the official source for that passport, not from an agent's summary, and we keep the page name and the date we read it.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>3. A legal reviewer signs the wording</h3>
        <p>Before a condition is written to you or published here, a named reviewer checks the wording and records when it was checked.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>4. The same source is read again before you fly</h3>
        <p>Rules can change between booking and departure, so the source is checked again close to your travel date and anything that changed is sent to you.</p>
      </li>
    </ol>
  </div>
</section>

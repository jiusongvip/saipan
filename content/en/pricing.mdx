    <h2>Published price ranges</h2>
    <p>A range is useful only with its context, so every figure is published together with currency, the period it applies to and the date it was last checked.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="price_ranges" />

    <h2>What is included and what is not</h2>
    <p>Two patients on the same program can pay differently: medication dose, number of monitoring visits and whether add-on techniques are used all change the scope. A personal quotation lists them line by line.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="included_excluded" />

    <h2>Possible add-on items</h2>
    <p>Whether an add-on enters your cycle depends on the physician's assessment and your response that month. This section lists the items that can arise and how each is charged.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="add_ons" />

    <h2>Accommodation, interpretation and transfers</h2>
    <p>Non-medical costs are not part of the medical quotation but they shape the whole budget. This section explains how they are estimated and who arranges them.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="non_medical" />

    <h2>Payment methods</h2>
    <p>Accepted payment methods, installments and receipts affect how you plan funds, so it is worth confirming them before your dates are fixed.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="payment" />

    <h2>Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds</h2>
    <p>Cross-border care moves with visas, flight seats and cycle timing. This section covers notice periods, how fees are handled, and refund conditions.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="refund_policy" />

    <h2>Currency, validity and last-verified date</h2>
    <p>Amounts are meaningless without currency and a validity period; both are shown next to the figure and its check date.</p>
    <FactSlot lang="en" slot="currency_validity" />

    <p class="source-note">The final figure is issued after clinical assessment and lists what is included, the currency and how long the quotation stands.</p>

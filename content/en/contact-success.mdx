    <h2>Your inquiry has been received</h2>
    <p>Our staff reply in the channel and language you chose, starting with the basics and the program you are considering, then arranging the physician's assessment.</p>
    <p>If timing matters to you, note a convenient call window in the free-text field, together with when you submitted the form and which country or region you are in, so we can match your record quickly.</p>

    <h2>What happens next</h2>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li>
        <h3>1. We check the basics with you</h3>
        <p>Staff confirm your name, the channel you chose and the program you are considering, and may ask a few background questions. No medical records are needed at this stage.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>2. A physician reviews your situation</h3>
        <p>The physician decides whether a Saipan plan fits and how many trips it would take, then replies with that assessment.</p>
      </li>
      <li>
        <h3>3. Only then, travel and costs</h3>
        <p>Once the direction is agreed, entry conditions, stay schedule and an item-by-item quotation are worked through. This site publishes no figures without a named source and a check date.</p>
      </li>
    </ol>

    <p class="source-note">To protect your privacy, please do not send medical record numbers, diagnoses or test reports in these messages.</p>

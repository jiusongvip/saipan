## Brief and constraints

Preserve rich content, regular alignment, the adjustable illustrative budget, and existing Astro routing. All financial examples remain explicitly fictional. Medical and lifestyle stock photography remains illustrative, not clinic or patient evidence.

Subject inventory: Saipan lagoon photography; fertility-visit preparation; Chinese and Traditional Chinese copy; English variant; itemized medical placeholder; nightly accommodation costs; separately counted travel days; companion preparation; document checklist; existing four-theme island photo carousel.

Argument: identify the service and place → select a preparation goal → calculate an illustrative budget → organize the travel sequence → understand medical questions → review island daily life → prepare a checklist → ask practical questions → contact.

## Direction
Dealt options: place-carries-it, uneven-photographs, split-stage. Selected split-stage, seed 521523732. Its calm image/text contrast fits the island setting. Kept the established palette and type roles. Adapted the direction to the explicit existing-layout constraints: no all-page sticky image, no full-screen hero and no horizontal drag navigation. No new photographs were taken from the dealt atmosphere set; existing project images already provide relevant material. No external stock, testimonial, clinician or performance statistic was added.

Idea: the Saipan visit becomes a readable preparation itinerary, with island photography supplying atmosphere and the budget supplying the live demonstration.

- Preserve the actual budget calculator and use an ordered travel list.
- Unify photographic crops, retain descriptive captions, and add restrained crossfade/settling motion with reduced-motion fallback.
- Run section entrances through progressive Web Animations; content remains visible if the animation module fails.
- Remove inherited reveal hiding from homepage contact and footer content.
